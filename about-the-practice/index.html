Microscopic Precision. Maximum Results.

In the world of modern medicine, precision is everything. The difference between a good outcome and an exceptional one often comes down to the smallest details—details that the human eye alone cannot always see.

This is why we incorporate microscope-assisted surgery, a state-of-the-art technique that enhances surgical accuracy to a previously unimaginable level.

By using high-powered magnification, microscope-assisted surgery allows for incredibly delicate procedures with unmatched precision.

Whether performing soft tissue grafting, implant placement, or esthetic gum contouring, the ability to work at a microscopic level means that only the necessary tissue is treated—leading to minimal trauma, reduced swelling, and significantly faster healing times.

Traditional surgical methods, while effective, rely on the unaided eye or basic magnification, which can sometimes result in unintended damage to surrounding tissue.

In contrast, microscope-assisted surgery ensures that every movement is guided by crystal-clear visualization, allowing for a more conservative approach that protects healthy tissue.

This translates to less discomfort, shorter recovery periods, and esthetic results that look incredibly natural for patients.

Imagine trying to thread a needle in dim lighting versus doing it under a high-powered magnifying glass with perfect illumination.

One approach is filled with guesswork, while the other offers complete control and accuracy.

This is the difference that microscope-assisted surgery makes in periodontal and esthetic procedures.

At Dr. Hutton's practice, technology isn't just about having the latest tools—it's about using them to create better patient experiences and more predictable results.

Gum Disease & Soft Tissue Health

Gum disease affects more than just your smile—it impacts your overall health.

We offer both non-surgical and surgical solutions to restore gum health, prevent further damage, and protect your teeth and bone structure.

  • Periodontal Disease Treatment – Comprehensive care for gingivitis and advanced periodontitis to halt disease progression.
  • Laser-Assisted Therapy – A minimally invasive approach that promotes faster healing and less discomfort.
  • Gum Recession Correction – Microsurgical soft tissue grafting to restore lost gum tissue and protect tooth roots.

Dental Implants & Full-Mouth Rehabilitation

Missing teeth don't just affect appearance—they impact chewing, speech, and long-term oral health.

  • Single & Multiple Dental Implants – Permanent replacements that function just like natural teeth.
  • Full-Arch Solutions (All-on-4, Pterygoid Implants) – Advanced options for severe bone loss or full-mouth restoration.
  • Bone Grafting & Ridge Augmentation – Regenerative treatments to rebuild lost bone and prepare for successful implant placement.

Esthetic & Reconstructive Periodontal Surgery
