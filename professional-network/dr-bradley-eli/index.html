Dr. Bradley Eli, DMD, MS

Orofacial Pain Specialist

At the forefront of orofacial pain management and dental sleep medicine, Dr. Bradley Eli is dedicated to helping patients break free from the cycle of chronic head, neck, and facial pain.

As a board-certified orofacial pain specialist, Dr. Eli brings over two decades of experience treating conditions like TMJ disorders, trigeminal neuralgia, myofascial pain, and sleep-disordered breathing.

His care blends advanced therapies—such as oral appliance therapy, precision nerve blocks, and Botox injections—with a deeply personalized approach that prioritizes each patient's comfort and long-term well-being.

Referring providers trust Dr. Eli for his ability to unravel complex cases and create thoughtful, patient-centered solutions.

From the moment they walk through the doors of Facial Pain Specialists, patients are met with a warm, supportive team committed to helping them regain function, confidence, and a life free from pain.
